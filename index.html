About Me

I'm a senior at the University of Michigan College of Engineering studying Computer Science, with a minor in UX Design. I'm especially interested in product work: problems that sit at the intersection of users, business, data, design, and technology. I like taking questions that are not clearly defined, figuring out what is actually happening through research and data, and working across disciplines to turn those insights into products and improvements.

Originally from Scottsdale, Arizona, I came to Michigan to grow beyond my comfort zone. I do my best work when I can move from ambiguity to a point of view, then collaborate with designers, engineers, and teammates to ship something real and learn from what happens next.

Outside of class, I stay inspired through weightlifting, running, and sewing. Those habits keep me disciplined, and they give me a creative outlet that is not a screen.

Product · Data · AI

Improving the VR Trial Experience

Axon Enterprise · Product Management Intern, Summer 2026

First-time VR trial users were hitting friction in setup and in-headset navigation, and the team needed a clearer picture of what was getting in the way of a successful evaluation. I was the intern on the Virtual Reality product team responsible for investigating that trial experience and turning findings into recommendations.

I mapped the customer journey, looked at usage and return data, and used those patterns to pinpoint onboarding and navigation pain points. In parallel, I built AI-powered internal tools to speed up proposal review and keep customer-facing claims current. The work stayed at the level of product insight: what users were struggling with, what the data supported, and what was worth changing next.

The analysis identified 420+ devices that did not need to stay active, uncovering about $30K in annual savings, and produced recommendations for reporting and trial-experience improvements. Full internship details are on my resume.
